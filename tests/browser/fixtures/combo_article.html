A gentle guide to walking every day

By A. Writer, 3 March

Walking every day helps the heart, the joints and the mind. Doctors suggest about thirty minutes, split into short walks if that is easier. Good shoes, a steady pace and a route with benches make it pleasant. Many towns have walking groups that meet in the morning, and they are a good way to make friends.
