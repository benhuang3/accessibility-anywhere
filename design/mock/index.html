One calm voice for every moment

Ally's bear sits in the corner of the screen and speaks up only when it matters. The card is the bear's speech: one surface, one button style, and urgency carried by words and the bear's face rather than loud colour.

Only three things change with urgency: the status line, the bear's expression, and whether Ally waits for an answer.

The rules behind it

One surface
White card, soft shadow, no outlines. The corner nearest the bear is tight, so the card reads as the bear's speech.
Urgency in words
A status line ("Safety check", "Worth a look") and the bear's face set the tone. Amber appears only in that line.
One button language
Teal for the safe choice, a quiet tinted button for the other. 64 px tall, 14 px corners, a verb on every button (“Close it”, “Keep it open”).
Extras stay quiet
Calling a helper and "Why?" live in a footer as text actions, so the main decision is never crowded.
